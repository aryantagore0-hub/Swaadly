Quick Kitchen Recipe

Peanut Butter Banana Smoothie

Thick, nutty, and ready in minutes using Swaadly Classic Crunch—great for mornings, study breaks, or post-workout fuel.

Paired Poster

Classic Original Splash

Ingredients

  • 1 frozen banana
  • 2 tbsp Swaadly Classic Crunch
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp rolled oats + pinch of cinnamon

Preparation Steps

  1. 1 Add banana, peanut butter, milk, oats, and cinnamon to a blender.
  2. 2 Blend on high until smooth, frothy, and creamy.
  3. 3 Pour into a glass, add a light crunch topping, and serve chilled.
