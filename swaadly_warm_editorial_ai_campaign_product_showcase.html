Peanut Butter Banana Smoothie

A quick, creamy breakfast or post-snack blend built around Swaadly Classic Crunch—zero cooking hassle and rich nutty texture.

Featured Jar

Classic Original (500g)

Ingredients

  • 1 frozen banana
  • 2 tbsp Swaadly Classic Crunch
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp oats + pinch of cinnamon

Steps to Make

  1. 1 Add everything to a blender.
  2. 2 Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. 3 Pour, top with granola + get on with your day.
